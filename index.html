Reggae and Soul

Our Vision

Reggae and Soul is a luxury Caribbean dining destination inspired by the immersive storytelling of Royal Caribbean, the wonder of Walt Disney, and the elegance of Sandals Resorts. Rooted in the symbolism of royalty and infused with Caribbean rhythm, Reggae and Soul blends fine dining, culture, and celebration into a single unforgettable experience.

Located in Homestead, Florida, next to Waterstone, Reggae and Soul is designed to become a regional landmark— a place guests don’t simply dine, but enter.

About Reggae and Soul

Reggae and Soul is not a themed restaurant—it is a living kingdom where Caribbean heritage, fine dining, and luxury hospitality converge. Drawing inspiration from English royal tradition and island vibrancy, the space is designed to feel prestigious yet relaxed, ceremonial yet soulful.

Purpose and Experience

Our goal is to redefine dining as a destination experience. Guests are invited to:

  • Enjoy five-star Jamaican and soul food cuisine
  • Immerse themselves in a royal Caribbean atmosphere
  • Celebrate culture, music, and community
  • Create lasting memories beyond the table

Arrival Experience

As guests travel down 8th Street, the lion emblem signals entry into something extraordinary. Valet-only service ensures elegance and ease from the first moment.

Ground Floor: The Royal Welcome

The ground floor introduces guests to the heart of the kingdom.

The Throne Room

At the center of the space stands the Throne, adorned in navy blue, royal blue, tan, and platinum. Guests are welcomed into the kingdom with ceremonial presence and visual grandeur. Beside the throne are royal garments, a crown, and scepter—allowing guests to capture photos and embody the spirit of royalty.

A striking blue-and-yellow macaw brings vibrancy and authenticity to the space, reinforcing the living Caribbean atmosphere. Four grand staircases rise from each corner of the building, each pillar flying the lion’s flag—symbolizing unity, strength, and sovereignty.

Top Floor: Rhythm and Leisure

The top floor transitions from dining into celebration and relaxation.

Entertainment and Lounge

  • Dance floor with evening reggae and soul
  • Relaxation lounge
  • Ice cream bar
  • Kids bar with tropical mocktails
  • Adult bar with premium Caribbean cocktails

A large-screen display sets the tone throughout the day—tranquil by daylight, rhythmic and vibrant by night.

Outdoor Grounds

The exterior grounds extend the Reggae and Soul experience into nature and play.

Gardens and Activities

The grounds are lush and productive, featuring:

  • Fruit trees: coconuts, bananas, strawberries
  • Putt-putt mini golf
  • Landscaped Caribbean walkways

This outdoor environment reinforces Reggae and Soul as not just a restaurant, but a place to linger, explore, and enjoy. Vehicles drop guests at the entrance before discreetly routing to the rear, preserving serenity and prestige.

The Menu

Fine dining Jamaican and soul food—elevated, authentic, unforgettable.

Signature offerings include:

  • Premium steak selections, grilled and finished with Caribbean spice blends
  • Fresh lobster, sourced on-site or from trusted suppliers, prepared multiple ways
  • Escovitch fish with crisp vegetables and vibrant pickling
  • Whole red snapper, fried or grilled to perfection
  • Fall-off-the-bone oxtail, slow-braised and deeply seasoned
  • Aromatic curry goat, rich and traditional
  • Classic soul food accompaniments such as mashed potatoes, candied yams, rice and peas, and seasonal vegetables

An outdoor drive-thru offers patties and coco bread, with a dedicated valet lane for seamless service.

Uniform and Workforce

Every detail reflects refinement, culture, and pride.

At Reggae and Soul, the experience is elevated not only by design and cuisine, but by the people who bring it to life. The workforce is intentionally sourced from Jamaica, ensuring that the food, service, and cultural presence remain authentic at every level.

Garden staff tend fruit trees, harvest produce, and interact organically with the space, reinforcing the sense that Reggae and Soul is alive and rooted in tradition.

All staff wear tailored navy blue rayon uniforms, designed to reflect unity, professionalism, and elegance while remaining breathable and practical for a tropical environment.

Enter the Kingdom

Reggae and Soul is where royalty meets rhythm, where fine dining meets culture, and where every guest is treated like a king or queen.
